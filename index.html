Privacy policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Echo is a personal tracker for food, protein, water, steps, weight and workouts. This policy explains what Echo stores and why.

What Echo stores

  • Account: your Apple sign-in identifier and, if you choose to share it, your email address.
  • What you log: foods and meals, calories and protein, water, steps, weight, workouts and your goals.
  • Photos of meals: only when you choose to estimate a meal from a photo. Photos are sent to the estimate service and are not stored after the estimate is returned.
  • Steps from Apple Health (only if you connect it): Echo reads your daily step count from Apple Health to fill your steps ring. Echo only reads steps, never writes to Apple Health, and never uses Health data for ads or shares it with anyone.
  • Product photos for stickers: turned into a sticker on your iPhone itself. The photo is not uploaded.
  • Social features (only if you use them): your name, profile photo and short bio; friends and the partner you connect with; notes and reactions you send; groups you join and the moments you post in them (including photos you choose to share); your current phase week and when you start or finish a workout, so friends can see it.
  • Without an account: if you choose “Not now”, everything stays on your iPhone only and nothing is sent to our servers.

Who can see what

Your logs are private to you. Friends see only what Echo shows on your profile (your rings for today, phase week, profile photo and bio). Food is never shown on your profile; a food photo is seen only by the group or friend you send it to. You can remove friends, leave groups and delete moments at any time.

How it is used

Your data is used only to show your progress inside Echo and to keep it in sync between your devices. Echo does not sell your data, does not show ads and does not use your data for tracking.

Who processes it

  • Supabase stores your account, logs and social content.
  • Anthropic (Claude) receives a meal photo or description when you ask for an estimate. It returns calories and protein.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account and everything you logged at any time in Echo: Profile › Delete account. You can also download a copy of your data in Profile › Download my data. Deletion is permanent.

Age

Echo is for adults 18 and over.

Health information

Echo is not a medical device and does not give medical advice. Estimates of calories and protein are approximate.
